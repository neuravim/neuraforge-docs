# Glossary

Agent, Retex, Quality gate... NeuraForge has its own vocabulary. This public glossary explains the user-facing concepts without exposing internal implementation contracts.

---

## A

**ADR** — Architecture Decision Record. A document that records a significant architectural decision.

**Agent** — AI role specialized in part of the delivery flow, such as analysis, planning, implementation, security, QA or documentation.

**Artifact** — Work product generated during a NeuraForge workflow, such as a brief, spec, review or operational note.

## B

**Brief** — Discovery summary that captures the request, context, risks and constraints.

## C

**Circuit breaker** — Safety mechanism that stops repeated failures and asks for human attention.

**NeuraForge** — AI-driven engineering framework for orchestrating specialized agents from the CLI.

## E

**Enterprise** — The most controlled mode, intended for sensitive work, production readiness, documentation and human approval.

## F

**Feature** — Unit of work handled by NeuraForge.

**Flash** — Fast mode for small, low-risk changes.

## L

**Lead** — The coordinating role that keeps the workflow aligned with the request.

## M

**Mode** — Workflow level: `flash`, `standard` or `enterprise`.

## P

**Provider** — AI backend or tool used by NeuraForge, such as Claude Code, Gemini, OpenCode, Copilot, Codex or a custom API.

## Q

**Quality gate** — Verification checkpoint used to avoid treating incomplete work as finished.

## R

**Retex** — Experience feedback used to improve future work.

**Routing** — Provider selection based on team configuration and availability.

## S

**Sanitizer** — Safety layer intended to reduce accidental exposure of sensitive data.

**Skill** — Reusable package of team guidance for a domain, workflow or standard.

**Spec** — Lightweight specification that describes expected behavior and acceptance criteria.

**Standard** — Default mode for normal feature work.

**Story** — Work item with expected behavior and acceptance criteria.

## T

**Token budget** — Cost and usage guardrail for AI workflows.
