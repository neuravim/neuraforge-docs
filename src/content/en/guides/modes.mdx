# The 3 NeuraForge modes

> Bug fix → Flash. Feature → Standard. Critical migration → Enterprise. When in doubt, start with Standard.

NeuraForge adapts the workflow to the risk and size of the task. You do not need the same process for a typo, a new API, and a regulated migration.

## At a glance

| Need | Recommended mode | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| Small fix or quick change | Flash | Fast implementation with minimal ceremony |
| Normal product feature | Standard | Analysis, plan, implementation and review |
| Sensitive or business-critical work | Enterprise | Stronger controls, human approval and operational documentation |

## Flash

Use Flash when the change is small, isolated and low risk.

```bash
neuraforge flash "Fix the broken health-check response"
```

Good fit:

- typo or small bug fix;
- simple refactor;
- limited scope;
- no major architecture or security impact.

## Standard

Use Standard for the majority of normal engineering work.

```bash
neuraforge standard "Add a users API with tests"
```

Good fit:

- new feature;
- integration;
- medium refactor;
- work that benefits from a plan and review.

Standard answers a practical need: deliver a reliable feature without turning every ticket into a governance program. The workflow keeps analysis, planning, implementation, tests, security review and QA connected, so the team can verify that the product request actually became a usable change.

## Enterprise

Use Enterprise when delivery quality matters as much as speed.

```bash
neuraforge enterprise "Migrate authentication to the new identity provider"
```

Good fit:

- critical migration;
- compliance or security-sensitive work;
- production-impacting change;
- stakeholder approval required;
- runbooks, rollback and support handover expected.

Enterprise mode is designed to avoid a common failure mode of AI coding tools: confusing a convincing answer or local prototype with a production-ready deliverable.

## Standard vs Enterprise

The difference is not only which agents are active. It is mainly the level of assurance expected around the deliverable.

### Standard: controlled feature delivery

Use Standard when you want structured delivery inside a real repository: analysis, plan, code, tests, review and acceptance criteria stay connected. It is the right fit for an API, an integration, a product change or a medium refactor that must be verified without requiring the full governance of a critical change.

### Enterprise: governed delivery

Use Enterprise when failure is expensive: production impact, security, compliance, migration, legacy debt or business approval. This mode adds what large organizations expect: operational documentation, runbook, rollback thinking, human approval, auditability and production readiness.

| Dimension | Standard | Enterprise |
|---|---|---|
| Goal | Deliver a reliable feature | Deliver a critical and defensible change |
| Typical use | API, product feature, integration, medium refactor | Migration, authentication, security, compliance, production |
| Documentation | Useful but lightweight | Expected: docs, runbook, rollback, support handover |
| Human approval | Optional, depending on the team | Expected or required |
| Security and QA | Standard review and verified criteria | Stronger controls and production readiness |
| Traceability | Feature artifacts | Artifacts, decisions, approvals and audit trail |
| Audience | Product and engineering teams | CTO, security, ops, compliance, large enterprises |

In short: **Standard** structures the delivery of a serious feature. **Enterprise** adds the governance needed when the feature must be operable, documented, auditable and acceptable for an organization with a high bar.

## Switching when the scope changes

If a task turns out to be more complex than expected, NeuraForge can guide you toward a stronger workflow rather than forcing you to restart from scratch. The exact orchestration logic is internal, but the user experience is simple: start with the right intent, then escalate when the risk justifies it.
